# Hi, I'm Nirjal Bhurtel

I'm an **Embedded Software Architect** at [Yatri Motorcycles](https://yatrimotorcycles.com/) in Kathmandu, Nepal. I work on firmware and systems software for electric motorcycles — STM32/ESP32, CAN and ISO-TP communication, OTA updates, and automated hardware-in-the-loop testing.

I'm a computer engineer with experience in embedded systems, networking, distributed systems and software development. I care about building reliable, energy-efficient digital infrastructure and expanding access to technology in Nepal.

Previously, I was a software engineering intern at Intra2Net in Germany, co-founded Omnecal, and served as president of the Kathmandu University Computer Club. I also publish research on HPC I/O, container security, sensor networks and intelligent traffic control.
